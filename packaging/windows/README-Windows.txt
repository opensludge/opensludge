SLUDGE for Windows
==================

Open "Out Of Order.exe" to play Out Of Order. With OpenSLUDGE.exe you can
play any SLUDGE game. When you open it, you choose the data file of the
game. You can also drag a game data file onto OpenSLUDGE.exe.

Keyboard shortcuts
------------------
  Alt+Enter       switch between a window and full screen
  Alt+A           switch smoothing (antialiasing) on and off
  Alt+C           open the controller settings
  Control+click   right-click
  Alt+F4          quit

Game controllers
----------------
  Left stick or d-pad      move the pointer
  A                        left click
  B                        right click
  X                        Space
  Y                        Enter
  Left or right shoulder   jump to the previous or next thing you can click on
  Start                    Escape (usually the game menu)

To choose an action for each controller button, open the controller
settings with Alt+C on your keyboard, or on your controller with Share or
Create (PlayStation), View (Xbox), Minus (Nintendo Switch), or Back or
Select (other controllers). A, B, X and Y are the bottom, right, left and
top buttons, also on controllers with other labels. A game can have its
own mapping in controller.ini, next to its data file. In Out Of Order, you
open the inventory with X (Space), save the game as "Quicksave" with Y
(S), and make the speech slower or faster with the left and right triggers
(- and +).

We keep saved games and settings in a folder named after the game, in your
Application Data folder (%APPDATA%).

If Windows SmartScreen says that it protected your PC, click "More info",
then "Run anyway".
