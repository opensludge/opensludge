SLUDGE for macOS
================

Open Out Of Order.app to play Out Of Order. With OpenSLUDGE.app you can play
any SLUDGE game. When you open it, you choose the data file of the game.

Keyboard shortcuts
------------------
  Command+F or Option+Enter  switch between a window and full screen
  Option+A                   switch smoothing (antialiasing) on and off
  Option+C                   open the controller settings
  Control+click              right-click
  Command+Q                  quit

Game controllers
----------------
  Left stick or d-pad      move the pointer
  A                        left click
  B                        right click
  X                        Space
  Y                        Enter
  Left or right shoulder   jump to the previous or next thing you can click on
  Start                    Escape (usually the game menu)

To choose an action for each controller button, open the controller
settings with Option+C on your keyboard, or on your controller with Share
or Create (PlayStation), View (Xbox), Minus (Nintendo Switch), or Back or
Select (other controllers). A, B, X and Y are the bottom, right, left and
top buttons, also on controllers with other labels. A game can have its
own mapping in controller.ini, next to its data file. In Out Of Order, you
open the inventory with X (Space), save the game as "Quicksave" with Y
(S), and make the speech slower or faster with the left and right triggers
(- and +).

We keep saved games and settings in ~/Library/Application Support/SLUDGE.

If macOS says that the app can't be opened because it is from an
unidentified developer, or that it is damaged, Control-click the app,
choose Open, then choose Open again. On recent versions of macOS, you may
have to go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway"
instead.
