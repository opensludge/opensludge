OpenSLUDGE for macOS and Windows, with **Out Of Order** ready to play.

| Download | For |
|---|---|
| `OutOfOrder-…-macOS-universal.zip` | Out Of Order on any Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel, macOS 10.13 or later) |
| `OutOfOrder-…-macOS-apple-silicon.zip` | Out Of Order on Apple Silicon Macs only (macOS 11 or later) |
| `OutOfOrder-…-macOS-intel.zip` | Out Of Order on Intel Macs only (macOS 10.13 or later) |
| `OutOfOrder-…-windows-x64.zip` | Out Of Order on 64-bit Windows |
| `OutOfOrder-…-windows-x86.zip` | Out Of Order on 32-bit Windows, and on any Windows from Windows XP on, with OpenGL 2.0 graphics |
| `OpenSLUDGE-…-*.zip` | The engine alone, for playing other SLUDGE games |

If you are not sure which Mac download to take, take the universal one.

Unzip the download, then open the app. If macOS does not open it,
Control-click the app and choose Open, or go to System Settings > Privacy &
Security and click "Open Anyway".

Changes in the engine:

- There are native builds for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, and the game
  picture has the full Retina resolution.
- We ported the engine to SDL 3, and we play sound with SDL instead of
  OpenAL and Alure.
- You can resize the window. We keep the aspect ratio of the game in a
  window and in full screen.
- You can type with any keyboard layout.
- You can play with a game controller. With the left stick or the d-pad,
  you move the pointer, A and B are the left and right mouse buttons, and
  with the shoulder buttons, you move the pointer to the things you can
  click on. In the controller settings, you choose an action for each
  button, including keys such as Space. You open them with Alt+C (Option+C
  on a Mac) on your keyboard, or on your controller with Share or Create
  (PlayStation), View (Xbox), Minus (Nintendo Switch), or Back or Select
  (other controllers). We show how to open them when you start using a
  controller.
- The mouse pointer moves smoothly, also in games with a low frame rate.
- On Windows, the colours are right on an HDR display in full screen too,
  and Windows can apply Auto HDR to the game.
